About

Science brain, business instincts.

I graduated from Monash University with a Bachelor of Science Advanced in Global Challenges with first-class Honours, the only degree of its kind in Australia. It pairs advanced scientific training with leadership, systems thinking and translational problem-solving, and it is where I learned to live at the intersection of science and business.

Since relocating to Copenhagen in 2025, I have been working hands-on in the European biotech landscape at Hervolution Therapeutics, where early-stage oncology research meets grant funding, consortium coordination and investor expectations. I like it best when all three collide.

Before that came an Honours research year at the Victorian Institute of Forensic Medicine, a string of internships across research, healthcare and policy, and seven months of travel that taught me more about adapting quickly than any classroom could.
